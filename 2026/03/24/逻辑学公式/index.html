逻辑学公式

Created2026-03-24|Updated2026-03-26|logic
|Word Count:353|Reading Time:2mins|Post Views:

公式

Conjunction Rules

Simplification (S)

$$ \begin{aligned} P \land Q \ \therefore\ P \end{aligned} $$

$$ \begin{aligned} P \land Q \ \therefore\ Q \end{aligned} $$

Adjunction (Adj)

$$ \begin{aligned} P \ Q \ \therefore\ P \land Q \end{aligned} $$

Disjunction Rules

Addition (Add)

$$ \begin{aligned} P \ \therefore\ P \lor Q \end{aligned} $$

$$ \begin{aligned} P \ \therefore\ Q \lor P \end{aligned} $$

Modus Tollendo Ponens (MTP)

$$ \begin{aligned} P \lor Q \ \neg P \ \therefore\ Q \end{aligned} $$

$$ \begin{aligned} P \lor Q \ \neg Q \ \therefore\ P \end{aligned} $$

Conditional Rules

Modus Ponens (MP)

$$ \begin{aligned} P \rightarrow Q \ P \ \therefore\ Q \end{aligned} $$

Modus Tollens (MT)

$$ \begin{aligned} P \rightarrow Q \ \neg Q \ \therefore\ \neg P \end{aligned} $$

Biconditional Rules

Biconditional to Conditional (BC)

$$ \begin{aligned} P \leftrightarrow Q \ \therefore\ P \rightarrow Q \end{aligned} $$

$$ \begin{aligned} P \leftrightarrow Q \ \therefore\ Q \rightarrow P \end{aligned} $$

Conditional to Biconditional (CB)

$$ \begin{aligned} P \rightarrow Q \ Q \rightarrow P \ \therefore\ P \leftrightarrow Q \end{aligned} $$

Negation Rules

Double Negation (DN)

$$ \begin{aligned} P \ \therefore\ \neg \neg P \end{aligned} $$

$$ \begin{aligned} \neg \neg P \ \therefore\ P \end{aligned} $$

Predicate Logic Rules

Universal Instantiation (UI)

$$ \begin{aligned} \forall x, P(x) \ \therefore\ P(a) \end{aligned} $$

Existential Instantiation (EI)

$$ \begin{aligned} \exists x, P(x) \ \therefore\ P(a) \end{aligned} $$

Universal Generalization (UG)

$$ \begin{aligned} P(a) \ \therefore\ \forall x, P(x) \end{aligned} $$

Existential Generalization (EG)

$$ \begin{aligned} P(a) \ \therefore\ \exists x, P(x) \end{aligned} $$

Quantifier Negation（QN）

$$ \neg \forall x ; P(x) \equiv \exists x ; \neg P(x) $$

Derived Rules

Negation of Conditional (NC)

$$ \begin{aligned} \neg (P \rightarrow Q) \ \therefore\ P \land \neg Q \end{aligned} $$

Conditional Disjunction (CDJ)

$$ \begin{aligned} P \rightarrow Q \ \therefore\ \neg P \lor Q \end{aligned} $$

Simplification of Conditional (SC)

$$ \begin{aligned} (P \land Q) \rightarrow R \ \therefore\ P \rightarrow R \end{aligned} $$

De Morgan (DM)

$$ \begin{aligned} \neg (P \land Q) \ \therefore\ \neg P \lor \neg Q \end{aligned} $$

$$ \begin{aligned} \neg (P \lor Q) \ \therefore\ \neg P \land \neg Q \end{aligned} $$

Negation of Biconditional (NB)

$$ \begin{aligned} \neg (P \leftrightarrow Q) \ \therefore\ (P \land \neg Q) \lor (\neg P \land Q) \end{aligned} $$
